Assertion (A): If the feasible region of an LPP is bounded, the objective function Z=ax+byZ = ax + byZ=ax+by has both a maximum and a minimum value on it. Reason (R): If the feasible region is bounded, the maximum and minimum values of ZZZ each occur at a corner point of the feasible region.

Tap the option you think is right.

Work it out on paper first. Then reveal the solution one step at a time.

Formula / idea Theorem 2 (NCERT): on a bounded feasible region, ZZZ has both a maximum and a minimum, each at a corner point.

  1. 1

    Assertion (A) is true: it is the first half of Theorem 2.

  2. 2

    Reason (R) is true: it is the second half of Theorem 2.

  3. 3

    A bounded region has finitely many corner points, so ZZZ takes finitely many values there.

    The largest and smallest of these are the maximum and minimum.

  4. 4

    So R tells us why the maximum and minimum exist: R correctly explains A.

    1
  5. 5

    Hence the correct option is (A).

Answer(A) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
